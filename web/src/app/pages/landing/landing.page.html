The problem

Paper trails break where projects get busy

  • Superseded drawings keep circulating by email and end up on site.
  • RFIs reference "the latest spec" instead of a specific revision and page.
  • Approvals live in inboxes, so nobody can say who accepted what, or when.
  • Overdue reviews are discovered only when the schedule slips.

The DocRoof approach

One register, enforced on the server

  • Revisions are immutable rows with checksums; the register always points at the current one.
  • RFIs pin the exact revision and page they cite, even after newer revisions arrive.
  • Workflow transitions are role-checked and written to an append-only audit trail.
  • A review queue view flags overdue items, and reminders are created automatically.

How the demo works

Your own sandbox, in one click

  1. Start. We create a private tenant from the seed template: two fictional projects and 15 synthetic documents.
  2. Switch personas. Act as requester, document controller, reviewers or project manager. The server authorizes every request by the active persona's project role.
  3. Follow the guided task. Open the HVAC specification, inspect its version history and work through the RFI.
  4. Reset or leave. "Reset my demo" rebuilds only your workspace; everything is deleted after 24 hours.
